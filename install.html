Legal Toolkit for Outlook

Adds a Legal Toolkit group to the ribbon when you write an email in Outlook.

Step 1: Download the installer file

Everyone needs this file, whichever option you use below.

Download installer file legal-toolkit-manifest.xml

Step 2a: Whole firm IT admin · recommended

Do this once. The toolkit then appears in everyone's Outlook automatically, on every computer (Mac and Windows) and in Outlook on the web. Nobody installs anything.

  1. Sign in to the Microsoft 365 admin center (admin.microsoft.com) as a Global or Exchange administrator.
  2. Go to Settings › Integrated apps and choose Upload custom apps.
  3. For app type choose Office Add-in, then Upload manifest file (.xml) from device, and pick the file from Step 1.
  4. Choose who gets it: Entire organization, or a group (for example a pilot group first).
  5. Accept the permission request (read and write the email being written) and press Finish deployment.

The ribbon buttons usually appear within a few hours, sometimes up to 24. People may need to quit and reopen Outlook.

Using Exchange Server on-premises instead?

In the Exchange admin center, go to Organization › Add-ins, press +, choose Add from file, pick the file from Step 1, then set it to Mandatory, always enabled or Optional, enabled by default. Note that on-premises Exchange doesn't support some of the newer attachment features.

Step 2b: Just for you about 1 minute

Use this to try it before IT rolls it out. It installs into your mailbox, so it then shows up on all your computers.

  1. In Outlook, on the Home tab, click Get Add-ins. (In some versions it's under … on the ribbon, or Tools › Get Add-ins.)
  2. Click My add-ins. Scroll down to Custom add-ins, click + Add a custom add-in, then Add from file…
  3. Choose the file from Step 1 and click Install.
  4. Open a new email. The Legal Toolkit buttons are on the Message tab.
Can't find Get Add-ins?

Open aka.ms/olksideload in a web browser and sign in with your work account. It opens the same add-ins window in Outlook on the web. Choose My add-ins › Add a custom add-in › Add from file…. It then appears in desktop Outlook too.

What's on the ribbon

  • AttachmentsReorder and rename, number as Exhibit A/B or Tab 1/2, add matter number or date, insert an attachment list.
  • FormatHeading, subheading, body text, bullets, numbered and (a)(b)(c) lists, quote block, all in the firm style.
  • MarkPrivileged & confidential, work product, settlement, without prejudice, draft; added to the subject and the top of the email.
  • MatterAdd the client-matter number to the subject or insert an "Our ref" line.
  • Pre-Send CheckOutside and look-alike recipients, forgotten attachments, unfinished blanks, and tracked changes or comments left in Word, Excel and PowerPoint files.

Privacy: the toolkit runs entirely inside Outlook on your computer. Email content and attachments are never sent anywhere.

Works in Outlook for Mac (classic and new), Outlook for Windows (Microsoft 365) and Outlook on the web, with a Microsoft 365 or Exchange work mailbox.
