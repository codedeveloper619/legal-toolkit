    Text styles

    Click where you want to write and choose a style, then type. Each time you choose a list style, the next item is numbered: (a), then (b), and so on. To restyle text that’s already there, select it first.

    Text colour

    Choose a colour and type, or select text first to colour it.

    Theme Colours

    Clean up

    Fixes stray fonts, sizes and colours from pasted text. Keeps bold, italics, links, theme colours and house styles. Your signature and the quoted thread below are left as they are.

    Legal markings

    Adds a banner to the top of the message. Choose a marking again to remove it.

    Quick inserts
